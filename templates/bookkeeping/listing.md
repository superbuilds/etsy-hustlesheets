## Description
Know exactly how much your business is making - and be ready for tax time - with one simple Google Sheet that does the math for you.

Built for Etsy sellers, makers, freelancers and small business owners. Record your sales and expenses in the ledger and everything else updates automatically: your dashboard, monthly Profit & Loss, Schedule C tax summary and quarterly tax set-asides.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Business Dashboard - pick a month or the full year to see net sales, gross profit, expenses, net profit, profit margin and how much to set aside for taxes, plus your top expenses, account balances and charts
- Ledger - dropdown categories and accounts; expense rows missing a receipt are highlighted
- Profit & Loss - a monthly P&L statement (income, returns, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses, net profit and margin) that builds itself
- Tax Summary - year-to-date totals organized by Schedule C line, meals counted at 50%, and estimated tax set-asides for each IRS quarterly period
- Mileage Log - log business drives; the deduction flows to your tax summary
- Setup - customize up to 50 categories and 8 accounts (checking, credit card, PayPal, cash...)

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
- No formulas to touch - just type in the yellow cells
- Tracks cost of goods sold, so you see your true profit margin
- Owner pay and transfers between accounts are handled correctly - they don't count as expenses
- Works for any year - change one cell
- Sample data for a small candle shop shows how everything works

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the file
2. Upload it to your Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (free Google account)
3. Enter your business details on the Setup tab and start recording transactions
