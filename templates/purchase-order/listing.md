## Description
Create, track and send purchase orders from one simple app that lives right inside Google Sheets - no accounting software, no monthly fees.

This isn't a static form. It's an interactive purchasing app built into Google Sheets: create POs from a simple form, track every order from Sent to Received to Paid, see what's overdue, and save any PO as a professional PDF for your vendor. Everything saves to your sheet instantly.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Purchasing Dashboard - open orders, spend this month, unpaid balance, overdue deliveries, monthly spend chart, POs by status and top vendors
- PO Editor - pick a vendor, add line items, and subtotal, tax, shipping and total calculate automatically; PO numbers are assigned for you
- One-click status updates - Mark as Sent, Mark as Received (received quantities fill in automatically) and Mark as Paid, plus partial deliveries
- Orders list - search and filter by status, vendor, payment and date; overdue orders flagged in red
- Vendor Directory - contacts, payment terms, order history and total spend per vendor
- Printable POs - a clean, professional purchase order you can save as a PDF and email to your vendor
- Your company details, tax rate and PO number prefix on every order
- Quick Start Guide (PDF) with step-by-step instructions

PERFECT FOR
Small businesses, retailers, restaurants, contractors, makers, Etsy and Shopify sellers, office managers and anyone ordering from suppliers.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the Quick Start Guide PDF
2. Click "Make My Copy" - the template is copied to your Google Drive
3. Add your company details and vendors, then create your first PO

GOOD TO KNOW
- Works with a free Google account - no paid plan needed
- Best used in Chrome on a computer
- Share your copy with your team like any Google Sheet
- The app's layout is fixed; your company details, vendors, orders and line items are fully editable
- Google Sheets only - does not work in Excel. Need a simple printable PO form for Excel? See our Purchase Order Form Template listing.
