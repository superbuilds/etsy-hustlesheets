## Description
Plan your project, track every task and see exactly where things stand on an interactive Gantt chart that lives right inside Google Sheets - no subscriptions, no complicated software.

This isn't a static spreadsheet. It's an interactive timeline app built into Google Sheets: every task is a bar from start to end date, filled in as work progresses, grouped by phase, with a live "Today" line and red alerts for anything overdue. Update tasks from a simple form and everything saves to your sheet instantly.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Interactive Gantt Timeline - task bars grouped by phase, filled by % complete, with Week and Month zoom, a Today line and Jump to Today
- Overdue Alerts - late tasks are outlined in red automatically
- Table View - change status, bump progress with +/- buttons, reorder tasks, edit or delete
- Add / Edit Form - task, phase, owner, start and end dates, status and % complete; duration is calculated for you
- Live Stats - total tasks, completed, in progress, overdue and overall progress
- Search & filters - by status, phase and owner
- Weekly Gantt grid on the spreadsheet tab too
- Quick Start Guide (PDF) with step-by-step instructions

PERFECT FOR
Project managers, small business owners, agencies, freelancers, product and marketing teams, students and anyone running a project with deadlines.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the Quick Start Guide PDF
2. Click "Make My Copy" - the template is copied to your Google Drive
3. Add your tasks and dates - the timeline builds itself

GOOD TO KNOW
- Works with a free Google account - no paid plan needed

- Share your copy with your team like any Google Sheet

- The app's layout is fixed; all tasks, dates, owners and progress are fully editable
