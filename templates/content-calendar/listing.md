## Description
Plan, schedule and track all of your content in a drag-and-drop app that lives right inside Google Sheets - no subscriptions, no complicated software.

This isn't a plain spreadsheet. It's an interactive board built into Google Sheets: drag cards from Draft to Published, see your whole month on a calendar, and add new content with a simple form. Everything saves to your sheet instantly.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Kanban Board - drag and drop content through Draft, In Progress, Review, Scheduled and Published
- Content Calendar - every post on its date, color-coded by status, with month navigation
- Table View - search, sort and update status in one place
- Insights - channel mix, content pillars, pipeline funnel and team workload
- Quick-Add Form - add or edit content without typing into cells
- Filters & search - by content pillar, channel, status and owner
- Summary stats - total content, in progress, scheduled/published and ready rate
- Quick Start Guide (PDF) with step-by-step instructions

PERFECT FOR
Marketing teams, social media managers, content creators, bloggers, agencies, small business owners and anyone juggling content across Blog, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and Email.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the Quick Start Guide PDF
2. Click "Make My Copy" - the template is copied to your Google Drive
3. Open the board tab, delete the sample content and start planning

GOOD TO KNOW
- Works with a free Google account - no paid plan needed
- Best used in a web browser on a computer
- Share your copy with your team like any Google Sheet
- The board's layout is fixed; all of your content, dates and statuses are fully editable
- Does not work in Excel
