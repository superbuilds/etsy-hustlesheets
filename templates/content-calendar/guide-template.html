1

Make your copy

Click the button above while signed in to Google. Your own copy is saved to your Google Drive - rename it anything you like.

2

Open the board

At the bottom of the spreadsheet, click the Marketing Content Calendar Kanban Board tab. That's your app. The Content Calendar tab next to it is where every item is stored.

3

Add content

Click + New Content (or the + on any column or calendar day). Fill in the title, pillar, channel, date, owner and notes, then Add Item. It saves straight into your sheet.

HustleSheetsPage 1
4

Move it through your pipeline

Drag any card to a new column - Draft → In Progress → Review → Scheduled → Published - or change the status dropdown on the card. Use the pencil to edit and the trash can to delete.

5

Switch views

  • Calendar - every item on its target date, color-coded by status. Use the arrows to change months.
  • Table - search, sort by date or title, and update status in one place.
  • Insights - channel mix, content pillars, pipeline funnel and each person's workload.

Use the search box and filters (pillar, channel, status, owner) on any view.

6

Make it yours

The sample content shows how everything works. When you're ready, open the Content Calendar tab and delete the sample rows below the header row - keep the title, summary and header rows. Your board updates instantly.

Tip: The board works best in a web browser on a computer. On a phone, open your sheet in your mobile browser rather than the Sheets app.

FAQ

Do I need a paid Google plan? No - it works with a free Google account.

Can I share it with my team? Yes - share your copy like any Google Sheet. Anyone with edit access can use the board.

Can I change the board's design? The board's layout is fixed, but all of your content, dates and statuses are fully editable.
